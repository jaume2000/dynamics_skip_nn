Residual connections~\citep{he2016resnet} made very deep networks trainable by
letting each block add a correction to its input, $x\mapsto x+f(x)$.
That update has the form of one explicit Euler step of an
ODE~\citep{e2017proposal,haber2017stable,lu2018beyond}, a reading that inspired
Neural ODEs~\citep{chen2019neuralordinarydifferentialequations}.
Whether a \emph{trained} stack behaves as an integrator is debated:
\citet{queiruga2020continuousindepthneuralnetworks} already treat residual stages as
piecewise-constant in depth and argue that standard ResNets are not meaningful
integrators beyond the syntactic analogy;
\citet{sander2022residualneuralnetworksdiscretize} show that the gap to a Neural ODE
need not vanish unless the residual maps vary smoothly in depth;
\citet{cohen2021scaling} find that the deep limit can be an ODE, an SDE or neither;
and \citet{marion2024implicit} prove that a ResNet initialised as a Neural ODE
discretisation remains one under gradient flow in the large-depth limit.

We refine trained residual stages at inference and use the refined path as a probe.
Write $R$ for the number of applications of each block with step $\Delta t{=}1/R$, so
that the integration horizon $T{=}n$ of an $n$-block stage is kept and nothing is
retrained.
Convergence itself is not the claim: any Lipschitz residual field has a continuous
limit (\cref{app:random-init}).
What matters is that the limit stays usable and that the path is faithful to the
trained network.
Refined $128$-fold, ConvNeXt, Swin and ResNet lose less than \refineLossBound{}
ImageNet top-1 point, whereas changing $T$ collapses every network
(\cref{sec:usable-limit}): the horizon matters, the resolution does not.
On the untied ConvNeXt-T stage, Gram statistics of the block increments agree between
$R{=}1$ and $R{=}100$ to within $\gramFidelityTol$; on the tied stage the order is
kept while the path straightens ($\strTiedNative\!\to\!\strTied$).
The stage is a composition of one ODE per block, not one ODE with weights smooth in
depth: bilinear blending of consecutive block weights costs $\bilinearDropLow$ to
$\bilinearDropHigh$ points (\cref{app:bilinear}).
The piecewise-constant reading already appears in Queiruga et al.; our novelty is
empirical: modern networks already trained, without retraining, and what that flow
looks like.

The same probe separates two transports behind the algebra $x+f(x)$
(\cref{fig:teaser}).
In ConvNeXt-T the field switches at every block boundary, where the heading turns by
about $\turnMean^\circ$ ($\turnLow^\circ$ to $\turnHigh^\circ$), and the nine blocks write
nearly orthogonal, positively correlated directions (consecutive cosine $\cosUntied$,
against $\cosRandom$ for random blocks of matched strength and $\cosTied$ for the
tied stage) that no later block reverses: survival $s_k>0$ for every image and block,
so the opposed fraction $E{=}\EUntied$ (\cref{sec:nine-directions}).
Straightness sits at $\strUntied$, between random ($\strRandom$) and tied
($\strTied$); writing $d=\|x_T-x_0\|$, $C$ for the sum of block chords and $L$ for
arc length, straightness $= (d/C)\,(C/L)$ with $C/L{=}\intraChordArcUntied$, so the
detour is made between blocks.
Alignment of each increment with the destination rises from $\alignFirstUntied$ to
$\alignLastUntied$, and the half-progress time is $t_{50}{=}\tFiftyUntied$ against
$\tFiftyTied$ (tied) and $\tFiftyRandom$ (random).
This staircase is specific to ConvNeXt: Swin is coherent and the ResNets reverse
(\cref{app:arch-transport}).

Weight tying turns the stage into one autonomous flow with search, expansion and
refinement phases.
A frozen decoder reads that state much earlier: native prefixes give
$\prefixTiedThree\%$ top-1 at $t{=}3$ against $\prefixUntiedThree\%$, and
$\prefixTiedFive\%$ against $\prefixUntiedFive\%$ at $t{=}5$
(\cref{sec:buys-costs}).
The frozen decoder is the right measure of early exit without training extra heads;
a low score still does not imply that early features lack information.
Sharing costs $\accGapTied$ points with $\paramsTiedFracFewer\%$ fewer parameters
(single runs, unmatched recipes), and tripling the applications of the shared block
($27$ applications, same parameters) reaches only $\accTiedDTwentySeven\%$, recovering
none of the gap: the price looks like autonomy, not depth
(\cref{sec:weight_tied,sec:buys-costs}).

Our contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Refinement as a faithful probe} and the switched-flow picture of a
    trained residual stage (\cref{sec:method}).
    \item \textbf{Basis-independent transport statistics}, with a random baseline of
    matched strength and a straightness decomposition into between-block and
    within-block parts (\cref{sec:nine-directions}).
    \item \textbf{Weight tying as an autonomous, anytime flow}, whose accuracy cost
    is autonomy rather than depth (\cref{sec:weight_tied,sec:buys-costs}).
\end{itemize}
