A residual block $x + f(x)$ has the algebraic form of one Euler step of size~$1$.
That observation has been used to read residual networks as discretisations of an
ODE, but a trained stack need not be a \emph{smooth} field in depth:
\cref{fig:teaser} shows two ConvNeXt stage-3 transports of the same horizon $T{=}9$.
Sharing one block yields a single autonomous flow whose speed rises and falls as a
bell; independent blocks produce a new field every unit of time, with kinks and
speed jumps at every integer.
The rest of the paper makes that contrast quantitative---and asks how much of the
accuracy of the switched stack is already carried by the shared field.

Skip Connections-NN (SCNN) wanted to solve grad vanishing problem that VGG introduced. By defining $x = f(x) + x$ it induced some properties: Works in the same feature representation. P.e. A given channel
will have the same purpose in the next layer.

\citep{sander2022residualneuralnetworksdiscretize, queiruga2020continuousindepthneuralnetworks} says that resnets just have the algebraic form of Euler ODE solver, that if params are steps they won'r be ODEs.

we augmentate the resolution of the feature dynamics to study with better quality how X moves along the latent-space manifold in time / depth.
