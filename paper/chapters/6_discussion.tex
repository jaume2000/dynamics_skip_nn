\paragraph{Why does one field nearly match nine?}
We now have a partial answer.
The shared field reaches the decoder's operating region earlier
(\cref{sec:buys-costs}), and extra depth with the same field recovers none of the
$\accGapTied$-point gap (\cref{sec:weight_tied}).
A hypothesis, not a tested claim: what the untied stage buys is the freedom for the
field to depend on depth, not more applications of one map.
A shared block conditioned on $t$ would test that reading.
We do not claim that the untied directions are unnecessary, nor that the blocks compute
functions simpler than their parameter counts allow; that remains an untested
hypothesis for future work.

\paragraph{Refinement as a probe beyond ConvNeXt.}
Every architecture converges to a continuous limit at fixed $T$ that keeps the function
within $\gapTiedODE$ to $\gapRFiftyODE$ points, although it sits
$\angleTiedRefine^\circ$ to $\angleUntiedRefine^\circ$ from the native state
(\cref{app:solver-endpoints}); breaking the horizon instead collapses every network.
A trained stage is a sequence of one-step solves, each replaceable by a finer integral
of the same block, but not a Neural ODE with weights smooth in depth
(\cref{app:bilinear}).
The probe needs no retraining, the Gram statistics only need block increments and
prefix decoding only needs the trained downstream network, so all three apply to any
residual stream, including looped transformers~\citep{dehghani2019universal,giannou2023looped}
and the residual streams of large language models.

\paragraph{Toward mostly autonomous fields.}
The two regimes suggest a design space between them: a shared field with small
per-block corrections, or cycles of a few shared blocks, would interpolate between an
autonomous and a switched flow.
Related schemes exist for convolutional networks~\citep{savarese2019learning} and
transformers, through cyclic layer sharing~\citep{takase2021lessons} and shared layers
with low-rank updates~\citep{bae2024relaxed}.
Designing fields that are mostly autonomous with controlled non-autonomy is future
work; the coherence statistics place such models on the ladder from random to
autonomous and can test whether coherence and accuracy move together.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
All results are on ImageNet-1k classification.
The tied and untied stages are one pair of ConvNeXt-T runs, without seeds and with
different learning-rate schedules; the $27$-application tied run is likewise a single
run.
We describe how the two transports differ and give a partial account of the cost; we
do not claim a causal explanation of why the gap is only $\accGapTied$ points.
The channel basis is not privileged, so per-coordinate readings of the trajectories
are partly determined by the Gram matrix and partly by the basis
(\cref{app:per-coordinate}); we base our claims on Gram statistics only.
The metrics exclude the massive ConvNeXt channel and the Swin corner tokens, and we
have not tested whether the ReLU after the ResNet sum explains its negatively
correlated increments (\cref{app:arch-transport}).
The frozen decoder measures readiness for the trained head, not information content.
