\section{Refining Residual Stages}
\label{sec:method}

We read a residual vision network as a three-part map: stem, stage~1 and stage~2
encode the image into a latent state $x_0$; the deepest residual stage transports
$x_0$ to a state $x_T$; stage~4 and the head decode $x_T$ into logits.
We study that middle transport, stage~3 of ConvNeXt~\citep{liu2022convnext},
Swin~\citep{liu2021swin} and ResNet~\citep{he2016resnet}.
It holds most of the residual depth (nine of the eighteen blocks of ConvNeXt-T),
keeps one representation and spatial resolution and has enough blocks to study
dynamics.
